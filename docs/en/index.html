    Privacy
    No internet.No sign-up.No uploads.

    Everything you hang lives in one folder on your own computer, and Pegline doesn’t look anywhere else. It checks the clipboard only on your machine, so new screenshots can hang themselves; if you’d rather it didn’t, switch to “drag only” in settings. Full privacy policy →

    Why I made it

    I take a lot of screenshots: errors, mockups, chat logs, verification codes. I usually need them right away, can never find them afterwards, and my desktop is always littered with dozens of “Screenshot” files.

    So I strung a rope across the top of my screen and let them hang there for a while, like laundry on a balcony. When I need something, I just reach up and grab it. When I don’t, it can disappear on its own.

    The clothesline idea comes from Tendedero, an open-source macOS app by Alejandro Buján. Pegline is a from-scratch take for Windows and Mac inspired by it, with page-turning PDFs, ghost mode, left and right edges, and seven skins.

    — Tao, who makes Pegline

    Install help
    Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

    Pegline doesn’t have a paid Microsoft code-signing certificate yet, so Windows stops it once. Click “More info” → “Run anyway” to install. If your antivirus blocks it, choose “Allow” or “Trust this program”.

    Mac says the developer can’t be verified, or the app is damaged
    1. Drag Pegline.app into Applications.
    2. Right-click it → Open, then click Open again.
    3. Still blocked? Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click “Open Anyway”.
    4. If it says the app is damaged, open Terminal, paste xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Pegline.app and press Return.
    Which Mac version do I need?

    Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says “Chip Apple M1/M2/M3/M4”, get the Apple silicon version; if it says “Processor Intel”, get the Intel version.

    Is the app in English?

    Not yet. The menus and settings are in Chinese for now, but almost everything works by clicking and dragging, and the shortcuts above work the same.

    Does it snoop on my clipboard?

    It reads the clipboard only on your computer, to hang new screenshots and copied items. Nothing goes online. You can also switch to “screenshots only” or “drag only” in settings.

    How do I uninstall it?

    Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Pegline → Uninstall. Mac: drag Pegline.app to the Trash.
